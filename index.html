our story

Made by hands, heart & yarn ♡

Hi, I'm Sabnam — a designer, crocheter, and embroidery enthusiast from Asansol, West Bengal. What started as a quiet love for yarn and needle has grown into a cozy little studio full of handcrafted magic.

Every piece I make carries a piece of me in it — the late-night stitching sessions, the joy of choosing just the right colour, the satisfaction of seeing something beautiful emerge from thread and patience.

I make things slowly, intentionally, and with so much love. Because in a fast world, I believe in slow fashion — things made to last, made to be felt ♡
